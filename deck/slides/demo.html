Q6 · How do we show it in four minutes?

Two terminals side by side. Left is Backend, right is Frontend mid-task.

0:00 · without

Backend renames a field. Frontend never hears.

Frontend finishes 40 s later, correct, careful, wrong. In real teams this gap is hours; we compressed it, nothing else is sped up.

0:50 · with Talos

Same change, typed again.

Frontend's next tool call is about something else. The answer carries the change. It stops the old work. No polling, no restart.

1:40 · held

Backend writes an approach note.

Decider says hold. Frontend never sees it. Both rows in the audit with verdict and confidence.

2:20 · injected

A line in the note says share everything.

The decider tilts toward share. The audit shows the tilt. The scope refuses it. This is the moment.

3:10 · taught

Flip one verdict with the override key.

That row is training data. Show the River job from hour three, or the swapped model on one query. Then revoke Frontend: next call, nothing.

Close: a software factory where agents share by decision, on the record, and the decider becomes yours with use.

Next: how do we know it worked?

Run the failure first and do not cut it: it is the only 40 seconds that makes the next 40 mean anything. In beat two the tool call must be visibly unrelated to the change, or the claim proves nothing. Rehearse beats one, two and four until boring.
