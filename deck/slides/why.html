Why it matters beyond Sunday

Every team that runs more than one agent hits this wall in the first week.

For QM users

  • Rooms are the right design, and they stop at the wall
  • Talos attaches where QM already routes a room's memory out: no fork, no change to QM's access model
  • A governed path between rooms is what a software factory needs next

For any team of agents

  • Scoped tokens, an audit row per read and sub-second revoke are the floor, not the feature
  • The path between rooms is where the value is, and nothing has it yet
  • Works the same for two agents or ten

Against plain shared memory

  • Sharing everything is what leaks and what goes stale
  • Talos shares by decision, records it, and learns from the record
  • Own your intelligence means the thing deciding what crosses is yours too

Two rooms, two agents. When one changes something, the other knows on its next step, and only what it was meant to know.
