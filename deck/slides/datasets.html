Q4 · Where the training data comes from

Public rows for both labels exist. Nobody has pointed them at this decision.

MAST-Data, Hugging Face

1,642 multi-agent execution traces, binary labels for 14 failure modes, 66 MB, from the paper on slide 2.

→ verdict rows: traces flagged inter-agent misalignment are crossings that should have happened and did not

CommitSuite

63,533 Conventional-Commits-compliant commits from 243 repos, typed, with AST-level change info.

→ kind rows: BREAKING CHANGE = contract; feat / fix by scope = decision or noise

Commit messages, high quality

292,269 commits from 15 repos: subject, body, type, scope and a breaking flag, loads with one call.

→ kind rows in twenty minutes of pandas; the breaking flag is the contract label

Architecture Decision Records

ADR markdown files across public repos: context, options, decision, consequences. Count not verified.

→ kind rows for the decision class

CodeReviewer corpus, Microsoft

Review comments paired with diff hunks, plus the refined code; data on Zenodo.

→ verdict rows: a review comment is a verdict crossing to the owner

Our own audit log

data/audit.jsonl from the demo: every crossing with verdict, confidence, option order, override.

→ the held-out set. Private notes and noise have no public corpus; a few hundred hand-labeled rows come from here

Kaggle has nothing closer. The commit corpora give kind; MAST and code review give verdict; the demo gives the test. Full recipe in docs/finetune.md.
